# Example style guide

Write the way a good product team talks to a friend who just bought the product: plain, warm and
specific.

## Headlines

- On screens where someone takes an action, the headline says literally what they are doing
  ("Pair device", not "Is this yours?").
- No questions as headlines.

## Sentences

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Say what happened and what to do next. Errors are one sentence: the problem, then the fix.
- No invented metaphors and no hype. Never "seamless", "unlock", "magic", "game-changing".
- Never use em dashes; use a full stop, comma or colon.

## Words

- Use words people already know from signs and phone settings.
- Write numbers as digits.
